## 6. ROM and rest posture

Finger MCP 0-90, PIP 0-100, DIP 0-70/90; wrist flexion 80, extension 70, ulnar 30, radial 20 (AAOS). Relaxed finger
posture MCP 28, PIP 25, DIP 13 (Jpn J Ergon); cadaver 35 / 40 / 15.

Caveats: Santoso is an Indonesian sample (smaller than Western); Buryanov is closer to a Western 50th-percentile male.
MCP arch offsets and spacing are derived, not measured. Hand thickness at MC III and the CMC axis orientations in an
anatomical frame were not retrievable from open sources; Cooney's trapezium orientation is the only frame-referenced
number. Soft-tissue depths are posture-specific ultrasound / MRI values.
