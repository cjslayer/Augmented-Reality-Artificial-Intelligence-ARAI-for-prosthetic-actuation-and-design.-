# Human hand joint axes, ranges and proportions — literature survey (2026-09-22, read-only subagent, web sources)

Purpose: anchor the anatomical rebuild of the articulated hand's anchor frames (spike 3). "conv." = converted or derived
by the agent, "(unverified)" = only seen in a search excerpt.

## 1. Finger flexion axes, cascade, MCP abduction

| item | value | source |
|---|---|---|
| finger MCP/PIP/DIP flexion axes parallel? | No: "increasingly oblique from index to little"; each finger flexed alone points toward the scaphoid tubercle (Lister's cascade) | Haughton et al. 2012, Open Orthop J 6:43, doi:10.2174/1874325001206010043 |
| scatter of the cascade | pointers converge within a 2-3 cm radius of the scaphoid tubercle (30 volunteers) | Ashraf et al. 2009, Orthop Proc 91-B Supp I:96 |
| per-finger flexion-plane tilt vs the middle finger | not published as angles; geometric derivation (palm ~114 mm, MCP2-5 span 60-70 mm, tubercle under the index/middle column): little ~25 deg, ring ~15, middle ~3-5, index ~5 radial; total fan index-little ~30 deg (+-10) | derived from ANSUR II dims + Ashraf 2009 |
| axial rotation of the proximal phalanx during MCP flexion (4D-CT) | index supinates to 40 deg flexion then pronates; middle/ring/little supinate; helical axis translates | Ishii et al. 2020, Clin Biomech 80:105188, doi:10.1016/j.clinbiomech.2020.105188 |
| obliquity of MCP flexion axis vs metacarpal | qualitative: "neither axis is perpendicular to the phalanges"; IP axis parallel to the flexion crease | Hollister et al. 1995, Clin Orthop 320:188 |
| MCP abduction / adduction | total 25-30 deg, mostly abduction (~+-15-20 about neutral) | Kenhub (tertiary); Physiopedia (unverified) |

Caveats: cascade tilt angles and MCP spacing are geometric derivations, not measurements; Hollister's actual axis
angles are in paywalled full papers; Crisco's 124 deg is coordinate-system dependent; several PDFs (Buryanov & Kotiuk
2010, Lee & Jung 2014, Greiner 1991) were unreadable; AAOS CMC abduction and the per-finger supination figures came
only from search snippets.
